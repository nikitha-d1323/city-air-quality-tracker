## Ethics, bias and limitations
- Sparse monitoring: Coimbatore has very few official air quality stations for
  a city of ~2.5 million people, so station readings cannot represent every
  neighbourhood. Areas without sensors are invisible in the data.
- Modelled vs measured: city-level time series come from Open-Meteo/CAMS model
  data, not ground sensors. Station data are real readings but limited in number.
- Honest forecasting: results are compared against a naive baseline, and
  errors are reported in the original units.
- Accessibility: charts use a colourblind-safe blue/orange palette, and AQI is
  shown as both colour and text category.
- Health advice is general guidance, not medical advice.
